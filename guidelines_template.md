# Presentation Guidelines & Specifications

## Target Audience & Tone
- **Audience**: Executive leadership, clients, or team stakeholders.
- **Tone**: Professional, strategic, concise, and data-driven.

## Key Presentation Requirements
- Keep slides punchy: 3 to 4 clear bullet points per slide (avoid long walls of text).
- Highlight key metrics, quantitative outcomes, and business impact.
- Incorporate supporting evidence and comparisons from the reference documents.
- Include a dedicated "Next Steps / Action Items" slide at the end.

## Structure
1. Title & Context
2. Problem Statement / Background
3. Primary Findings & Analysis (from primary document)
4. Comparative Data & Supporting Evidence (from reference documents)
5. Strategic Recommendations & Roadmap
